# Masoretic Text — Westminster Leningrad Codex

Read the Hebrew and Biblical Aramaic **Masoretic Text** of the Old Testament, book by book. The electronic **Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLC)** is based on the Leningrad manuscript; it is not identical to a particular printed Hebrew edition used by the KJV translators.

**Text source:** [Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible](https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb) (WLC text: public domain). No morphology annotations are reproduced.

**Versification:** Original Masoretic chapter and verse numbering is retained; it sometimes differs from the KJV. No remapping was applied.

**Text:** Vowel points and cantillation marks are preserved; Ketiv/Qere variants are distinguished in notes following each book.

**Coverage:** 39 books · 929 chapters · 23,213 verses
