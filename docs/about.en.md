# About & Contribute

**Together Before Scripture · 성경 앞의 우리**

*Read Together, Ask Together.*

This is an open project for reading Scripture, examining evidence, and discussing questions. The editor believes the Bible is the Word of God but does not require visitors to share that faith.

## How to participate

- Listen to Bible readings and share thoughtful questions on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@GladBinah).
- As translated biblical books are published, review the corresponding translation notes and revision history.
- Submit significant corrections, alternative renderings, and evidence through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/GladBinah/gladbinah.github.io/issues).

## Transparency and privacy

The project identifies its progress and publication status, so unapproved drafts are not represented as finished translations. The editor's personal identity is not currently published.

The primary translation and commentary texts will be in Korean. Core project information is also available in English.
