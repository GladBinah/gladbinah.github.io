# Bible Translation

**A Korean translation of the KJV New Testament · Open review · Ongoing revision**

This project began with the need for a Korean KJV translation that could be used and shared freely for noncommercial purposes. Work will begin with the New Testament, with one Markdown translation file per biblical book and separate records for important translation decisions.

## Current status

The website and translation principles are being prepared. **No approved translation text has been published yet.** Once the first passage is reviewed and approved, its book, version, and coverage will appear in the [New Testament index](books/index.md).

## Process

1. Draft a Korean translation from the KJV for a coherent passage.
2. Compare significant choices with existing Korean KJV translations, refining wording, tone, paragraphs, and speech boundaries.
3. Consult the Greek Textus Receptus and lexical or grammatical evidence where important questions arise.
4. Discuss and approve a revised passage, then incorporate it into the relevant book.
5. Document significant decisions and track book-specific revisions and public criticism.

AI supports initial drafting and research comparisons; final decisions require human review. Neither perfection nor infallibility is claimed.

## Intended license

The independent Korean translation is intended for release under **CC BY-NC 4.0**, subject to review of ownership, applicable rights, and the precise licensing scope before publication. This license does not cover copyrighted passages from comparison translations.

## Questions and corrections

Significant translation decisions and revision histories will be public. Readers may propose corrections through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/GladBinah/gladbinah.github.io/issues).

**Note:** The biblical translation itself is primarily in Korean. The English pages explain the project rather than duplicating an English Bible edition.
