# Translation Principles — Working Draft

These principles are provisional and may be revised when the work reveals better evidence or a more appropriate procedure.

## 1. Base text and reference materials

- **Primary translation source:** The 1611 KJV translation tradition. The standard-spelling edition commonly associated with 1769 is used for practical reference, with relevant differences from the original 1611 printing identified.
- **Korean comparisons:** Standard King James Bible (Korean), the Korean King James Bible published by Bible Believers of Korea, and the Korean King James Authorized Version (흠정역).
- **Korean style:** The dignified, traditional register of the 1961 Korean Revised Version (개역한글), without copying protected creative expressions.
- **Greek consultation:** The Textus Receptus, particularly Stephanus 1550 as presented in Blue Letter Bible, is consulted where meaningful questions arise.

The KJV is an English translation, not the Greek original. Greek grammar and usage are considered where they materially affect an interpretive decision.

## 2. Translation and presentation

- Prepare an independent Korean draft from the KJV before comparing existing Korean translations.
- Preserve the KJV's dignified tone while remaining intelligible to Korean readers.
- Organize paragraphs around literary and semantic units, preserving chapter and verse references.
- Mark direct speech with quotation marks. Where the speaker or the boundary of a quotation is disputed, document the editorial judgment.
- Treat legitimate alternatives as alternatives rather than automatically labeling one an error.

## 3. Review and versioning

- Work proceeds passage by passage: **AI-assisted draft → discussion and review → approved text**.
- Each biblical book has a separate Markdown file and version number.
- Significant decisions include evidence, alternatives, and uncertainties.
- Constructive objections are documented, including consequential arguments that are ultimately not adopted.
- Previously published versions remain traceable through GitHub history.

## 4. Publication

The intended license for independently contributed translation material is **CC BY-NC 4.0**. The precise license and any exceptions will be stated when translation texts are released. Existing Korean translations retain their respective rights.

This is a Christian reader's open translation and learning project, not a claim of professional authority or infallibility.
