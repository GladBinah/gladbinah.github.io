# KJV Resources and Perspectives

This page introduces resources on the history of the KJV and intriguing numerical patterns observed in particular editions. **Listing a source does not imply endorsement of all its conclusions.** Observations are distinguished from interpretations of their significance.

## History of English Bible translation

- [Video on the history of English Bible translation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tw3kwH3Etfw) — An overview of the historical development of English Bible translation and the setting in which the KJV emerged.

The main reasons for choosing the KJV are its historical significance, confidence in the translators' work, continuity with the editor's prior study, and relative accessibility for reuse.

## Textual variants, trust in Scripture, and why I am not King James Only

- [Video on New Testament textual variants and the reliability of Scripture](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HdiCyzCvNzM) — Explores how the existence of manuscript variants can be reconciled with confidence in the New Testament's central message. It also relates to my reasons for not adopting a King James Only position.
- [Daniel B. Wallace — *How Can You Trust the New Testament When the Original Manuscripts Are Different?*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NikVdhp0YFs) — A discussion of New Testament textual variants and confidence in its manuscript transmission.

I acknowledge that textual variants exist and that some are important for interpreting individual passages, including 1 John 5:7–8 and Mark 16:9–20. But **a debated reading in a particular passage is not the same as the collapse of a central Christian doctrine**. I understand the central teachings about the Trinity, Christ's divinity, atonement, and resurrection to be supported across the New Testament, not by a single contested reading. This is why I respect the KJV without claiming that it alone preserves the true text. Other editions and translations remain open to serious examination on the evidence.

## Numerical patterns observed in the KJV

- [Truth is Christ — *The 1611th Mention of LORD (And Why It's a Really Big Deal)*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yS78mFJcvhQ) — Presents numerical correspondences involving word occurrences in the KJV and interprets their religious significance.
- [KJV Code — *Jesus' 666th Mention Is False Prophet Bar-jesus*](https://kjvcode.com/pattern/jesus-666th-mention-is-false-prophet/) — A numerical pattern and counting method linking the occurrence of a name in the KJV to Bar-jesus in Acts 13:6.

Such unexpected correspondences strike me as fascinating and raise, for personal reflection, the possibility of providence beyond the translators' own intentions. I do not, however, take these patterns as proof that the KJV is the only correct or an infallible translation. **The numerical observation and its theological interpretation are distinct.** A count can be checked against a specified text edition and counting rule; the existence of the pattern alone cannot establish a divine cause.

Translation decisions are made by examining the relevant English text and, when necessary, the Greek Textus Receptus under the project's [Translation Principles](principles.md).
