This section introduces our independent Korean Bible translation, organized by testament. Biblical texts and significant translation decisions will be published passage by passage after review and approval.

## Current Status

The current published edition is [Genesis 1:1–2:25 (Korean 0.2.2 / English 0.2.0)](../old-testament/genesis.md). It includes the independent Korean translation, English KJV Reading Edition, original-language study resources, and Translation Notes. New sections will be added after review and approval. No New Testament passages have yet been published.

The Korean-language view presents the independent Korean translation and the English-language view presents the separately reviewed KJV Reading Edition.

## Reading Conventions

Added parenthetical information in the Korean text is editorial supplementation for names, vocabulary or wordplay, not Scripture itself. Bold 주 represents the Hebrew divine name (יהוה); bold chapter and verse numbers and headings are structural markers. See the Translation Principles for details and examples.
