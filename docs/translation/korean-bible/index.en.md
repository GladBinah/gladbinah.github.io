# KJV Korean Bible

This section introduces our independent Korean Bible translation, organized by testament. Biblical texts and significant translation decisions will be published passage by passage after review and approval.

- [Old Testament](../old-testament/index.md)
- [New Testament](../new-testament/index.md)

## Current Status

The translation principles and reference Bible texts are already available on this website. The independent Korean translation and English KJV Reading Edition are still being prepared and reviewed, and **no independent Old or New Testament translation passages have yet been approved for publication**. The New Testament is the initial priority, while Old Testament work will proceed as a parallel, longer-term project. As passages are approved, this page will provide access to each book's publication coverage, version, and significant translation decisions.

When texts are published, the Korean-language pages will present the Korean translation, while the corresponding English-language pages will present the separately reviewed KJV Reading Edition.
