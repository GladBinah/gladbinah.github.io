# KJV Korean Bible

This section introduces our independent Korean Bible translation, organized by testament. Biblical texts and significant translation decisions will be published passage by passage after review and approval.

- [Old Testament](../old-testament/index.md)
- [New Testament](../new-testament/index.md)

## Current Status

Our first published edition is [Genesis 1:1–2:3 — Creation and the Seventh Day (0.1.0)](../old-testament/genesis.md). It includes the independent Korean translation, English KJV Reading Edition, original-language study resources, and Translation Notes. New sections will be added after review and approval. No New Testament passages have yet been published.

The Korean-language view presents the independent Korean translation and the English-language view presents the separately reviewed KJV Reading Edition.
