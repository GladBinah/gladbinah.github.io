# KJV Korean Bible: Old Testament

The Old Testament is translated book by book and section by section, with approved passages released as they are ready. Genesis 1:1–2:25 is currently available.

## Textual starting point

The **standard KJV 1769 English text and its underlying Masoretic Text (MT) tradition together serve as the primary translation standards** for the Old Testament, with the publicly available [Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLC)](../../source-texts/wlc/index.md) as a primary Hebrew reference. We generally follow KJV choices where the original grammar, vocabulary, and context permit; departures require adequate reasons. Where meaning materially conflicts, the original text takes priority. Where the **Septuagint (LXX)** or **Dead Sea Scrolls (DSS)** preserve materially different readings, we will compare textual evidence, original-language context, and interpretive consequences. Passages such as Deuteronomy 32:8 will receive particular attention, with significant alternatives and the reasons for textual decisions recorded.

KJV wording will be distinguished from proposed renderings based on alternative Hebrew readings. As individual sections are approved, the index will link to their **passage coverage, book-specific versions, revision records, and related commentary**.

!!! info "Gradual, book-by-book publication"
    No completion date or fixed order has been set for the Old Testament. Texts will be published only after review and approval. English KJV reading-edition changes and Korean translation changes will have separate version histories.
