# KJV Korean Bible: Old Testament

The Old Testament is translated book by book and section by section, with approved passages released as they are ready. Genesis 1:1–2:3 is the first published passage.

## Textual starting point

The **Masoretic Text (MT)** serves as the starting point for the Old Testament, with the publicly available [Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLC)](../../source-texts/wlc/index.md) as a primary Hebrew reference and the KJV as an important comparison translation. Where the **Septuagint (LXX)** or **Dead Sea Scrolls (DSS)** preserve materially different readings, we will compare textual evidence, original-language context, and interpretive consequences. Passages such as Deuteronomy 32:8 will receive particular attention, with significant alternatives and the reasons for textual decisions recorded.

KJV wording will be distinguished from proposed renderings based on alternative Hebrew readings. As individual sections are approved, the index will link to their **passage coverage, book-specific versions, revision records, and related commentary**.

!!! info "Gradual, book-by-book publication"
    No completion date or fixed order has been set for the Old Testament. Texts will be published only after review and approval. English KJV reading-edition changes and Korean translation changes will have separate version histories.
