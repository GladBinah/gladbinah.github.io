# KJV Korean Bible Commentary: Old Testament

The project will develop Korean commentaries on the 39 books of the Old Testament, beginning with the historical, grammatical, and literary meaning of each passage. Book introductions and passage commentaries will be published as they are reviewed and approved.

**No Old Testament commentary passages have yet been published.**
