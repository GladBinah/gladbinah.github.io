# KJV Korean Bible Commentary: New Testament

The project will develop Korean commentaries on the 27 books of the New Testament, first considering each passage's historical, grammatical, and literary meaning and then examining connections with the Old Testament and broader canonical or theological implications where appropriate. Book introductions and passage commentaries will be published progressively after review and approval.

**No New Testament commentary passages have yet been published.**
