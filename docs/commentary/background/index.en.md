# Background Resources

These resources introduce historical, cultural, literary, and textual background relevant to reading Scripture. They are grouped by testament. Listing a resource does not mean that this project endorses all of its claims.

- [Old Testament Background Resources](../references.md) — historical setting and relationships with ancient literature, including the Pentateuch and Joshua
- [New Testament Background Resources](../nt-resources.md) — historical and cultural context, manuscript transmission, and early Christianity

Additional resources will be included as they are reviewed.
