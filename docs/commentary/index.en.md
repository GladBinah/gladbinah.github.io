## Purpose of the Bible Commentary Project

I once left Christianity entirely but eventually returned to faith as I began reading Scripture again. The most significant change in that journey was how I read the Bible. I came to recognize that understanding its message requires attention to the historical and cultural settings of its human authors, the ways people in those times understood the world, and the audiences those authors first addressed. These contexts are not always fully explained within the biblical text itself. As I began examining them, many passages that had once seemed difficult or contradictory became clearer to me. I believe this helped me understand more faithfully what God and Jesus Christ intended to communicate.

I also came to see how easily the biblical authors' messages can be distorted when I read Scripture through my own values and select only the meanings I wish to find. Reading more attentively within its original contexts, I began to discern a coherent message across passages that had previously seemed disconnected or inconsistent. In that process, I discovered what I understand to be the distinctive teachings of Christianity and the beauty of its message. This understanding played an important part in my return to Christian faith.

Sometimes I wonder whether I might not have left Christianity in my younger years if someone had helped me read Scripture in this way. I am writing these commentaries to share the journey through which I came to understand the Bible differently and the message I found within it. I do not ask readers simply to accept my conclusions; rather, I hope to help them **understand what Scripture actually says and decide for themselves**. At the same time, I welcome questions and criticism that may expose weaknesses in my interpretations, sharpen discussions of disputed passages, and help me continue to understand Scripture more accurately.

## How the Commentaries Will Be Developed

To pursue this purpose, I will study Scripture book by book, dividing each text into meaningful reading units and gradually building a record of interpretation and discussion. I will begin with the historical, cultural, linguistic, and literary contexts of its authors and original audiences, establishing a passage's primary meaning before examining canonical connections and theological implications where relevant.

I will distinguish what the text explicitly states from my interpretations and inferences, and consider significant alternative views together with their evidence. These commentaries represent my current understanding rather than a final or unquestionable answer. When stronger evidence or well-founded criticism calls for revision, I will explain and record the changes.

The approach behind these commentaries is explained in the [Principles of Biblical Interpretation](principles.md). Relevant historical and cultural resources are listed under [Background Resources](background/index.md).

## Reading Scripture with the Commentary

Each commentary has its own structure: a book introduction, optional Part-level guidance and synthesis, Section-level summaries and exposition, and concluding remarks. The commentary remains independently readable, while its reading units are designed to display the relevant published Korean translation or English KJV Reading Edition. Where the project translation is unavailable, this will be indicated so readers can use their own Bibles. Scripture and commentary retain their own literary structures.

Where available, our published translation serves as the primary Korean reading and quotation edition. Relevant translation decisions are linked to Translation Notes. The translation and commentary retain independent versions and [rights policies](../policies/index.md); changes affecting quotations or arguments prompt review rather than automatic revisions of the commentary.
