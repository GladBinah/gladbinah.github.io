# KJV Korean Bible Commentary

This section introduces our book-by-book Korean Bible commentaries, organized by testament. Each commentary first examines the historical, grammatical, and literary meaning of a passage, then considers canonical and theological implications where warranted. Reviewed and approved reading units will be published progressively.
