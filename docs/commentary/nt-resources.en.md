# New Testament Background Resources

This page will collect selected references on the historical and cultural settings of the New Testament, its ancient sources and manuscript traditions, the Gospels, and the early church. Materials will be organized by biblical book or topic as the collection develops.

Readers can suggest relevant material through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/GladBinah/gladbinah.github.io/issues).

## Historical Bible Maps

- [ChurchMaps.info — Historical Bible Maps](https://churchmaps.info/) — Provides high-resolution maps covering New Testament Palestine and Paul's journeys, among other topics. This is a preferred reference for locating maps of the historical settings of both Testaments. The site states that all its maps and source files are released into the public domain and may be freely used, modified, and redistributed (verified November 25, 2026). For each use, we verify the map's title, version, source, and rights information and assess its chronology, ancient place identifications, and proposed routes against Scripture and other relevant historical and geographical evidence.
