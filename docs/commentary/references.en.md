# Old Testament Background Resources

This page collects selected resources on questions arising from the historical and literary contexts of Scripture.

## Pentateuch and Joshua — The Bible and Ancient Myths

- [Video examining claims that the Bible plagiarized earlier myths, covering the Pentateuch through Joshua](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DEvW36mZ0Kk) — Discusses how to evaluate claims that similarities between biblical narratives and other ancient myths indicate literary plagiarism, with a scope extending through the Book of Joshua.

The observation that ancient texts contain similar narratives should be distinguished from the historical conclusion that one text directly plagiarized another. Assessing such claims calls for attention to content, literary genre, chronology, specific textual parallels, and possible lines of transmission. This page serves as a reference list. Assessments of individual claims may be developed with supporting evidence in the relevant book-by-book commentaries.
